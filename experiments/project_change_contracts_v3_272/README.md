Exactly the same 12 known DEV cases as V2, object 272, pairs 5 and 7. No reserve,
third pair, full DEV, production operation or OpenRouter request.

A2 discovers source forms within the previously admitted pages. A native drawing
title and drawing layout identify a scheme; its functional scope remains bound
to the preexisting subject/page map. Tables cannot fulfill a scheme requirement.
Every requirement retains required/selected type, match, delivery and omission.
Mandatory primary states and graphic counterparts precede supporting material.
Unknown form or exhausted budget remains partial. This bounded native classifier
is not a claim of general document understanding or autonomous scope retrieval.

D2 records applicability before comparison, separates administrative stage and
functional subject from source labels, scopes conflicts to claim IDs, derives
cardinality from normalized members and evaluates change existence before
materiality. V2 replay adapts its explicit semantic no-change judgment without
inventing numeric parsing of narrative aggregates. V3 requests a structured
existence observation with validated OLD and NEW witnesses.

`prepare` rebuilds source packages and replays V2 responses against their ORIGINAL
V2 evidence. It never sends old packets or responses to inference. Truth is sealed
until all 12 new calls succeed. Evaluation labels occur only in tests/gates/report,
outside the filesystem allowlist of the isolated model process.


Freeze requires both A2 package regression and all 12 D2 replay gates to PASS.
Codex CLI / ChatGPT, gpt-6-astra / xhigh, isolated fresh process per case, one
prompt/config, eight rasters, sequential calls, no automatic retries or tuning.
Failed attempts stop the queue. Stop after 12 successes even when all targets pass.
